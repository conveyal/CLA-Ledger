**Provisional implementation draft — legal review pending.** This agreement is
intended for Conveyal LLC's organization-wide open-source projects, including
R5. It is adapted from the Cloud Security Alliance CLA-Ledger and is not legal
advice.

Thank you for contributing to Conveyal's open-source projects. This Contributor
License Agreement ("Agreement") records the rights you grant to Conveyal LLC
("Conveyal") for Contributions submitted to a covered project.

"You" means the individual natural person who submits a Contribution and
enters this Agreement, or an authorized representative signing a corporate
authorization on behalf of an organization. "Contribution" means any original
work of authorship, including modifications or additions, intentionally
submitted for inclusion in a covered Conveyal project. This includes source
code, documentation, data, schemas, tests, and configuration.

You represent that You are legally entitled to grant these rights, that Your
Contribution is Your original creation or is clearly identified as submitted

Contribution and grant these rights.

## 5. No warranty and notification

Contributions are provided "AS IS" without warranties, except where required
by law or agreed in writing. You agree to notify Conveyal of facts that would
make these representations inaccurate.

## 6. Electronic execution and acceptance

You offer to enter this Agreement by opening a signature pull request against
the central `conveyal/CLA-Ledger` repository that adds a record under
`signatures/` from the GitHub account named in that record. The Agreement
becomes effective only when Conveyal accepts the offer by merging the record.
The GitHub numeric account ID is the identity of record; the signature and
grant survive account renaming, transfer, or deletion.

Corporate authorization records additionally identify the organization,
authorized representative account, and covered contributor account IDs. A
corporate record is effective when Conveyal merges it.

## 7. Scope and versions

This Agreement covers present and future Contributions to Conveyal's covered
open-source projects, including projects later added to the organization-wide
program. It is forward-looking and does not re-bind historical contributions.
Material changes receive a new version and require a new record for future
Contributions; prior records and grants remain effective under their signed
version.

No governing-law or venue clause is included in this provisional version. Any
such clause will be introduced only through a counsel-reviewed material
version.
